# Privacy Policy — Bob Oura ChatGPT

Effective Date: October 28, 2026

Bob Oura ChatGPT is a personal, noncommercial application that connects the owner's Oura account to ChatGPT through a privately operated Cloudflare Worker.

The application accesses Oura health and fitness information authorized by the account owner, including sleep, readiness, heart rate, HRV, and activity data.

Oura authentication credentials and tokens are handled by the Cloudflare Worker. Data requested through the connector may be shared with ChatGPT to answer the owner's questions.

The application is intended solely for personal use and does not sell Oura data or use it for advertising.

The owner may revoke access through Oura's account settings and disconnect the connector from ChatGPT.
